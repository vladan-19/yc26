The first cycle of the “School of Ethics” of the Youth Caucus Ethics Forum “William C. McCoy”

The School of Ethics of the Youth Caucus Ethics Forum William C. McCoy is a traditional activity within the Youth Caucus association, organized by the Youth Caucus Ethics Forum William C. McCoy.

The aim of the School of Ethics is to bring young people closer to ethical reasoning, open perspectives for critically defining good and bad decisions as objective factors, incorporate ethical conduct into the business and consumer spheres, introduce them to the academic roots of ethics and moral philosophy, the concept of ethics in leadership, and its implementation in the education system and (digital) technologies.

The first cycle of the School of Ethics is being held from March to August 2025. The lecturers for the first cycle are Professor Dr. William C. McCoy and Mr. Bill Carlson.

Read more about the lecturers:

  • https://www.clemson.edu/ethics/about/staff.html
  • https://www.thebillcarlson.com/general-8

  1. March – Ethics in Private and Professional Decision-Making

  2. April – Ethics in Leadership and Business

  3. May – Foundations of Ethics and Moral Philosophy

  4. June - Ethics in Technology and Digital Spaces

  5. July - Ethics in Education and Training

  6. August – Ethics in Business and Consumer Behavior
